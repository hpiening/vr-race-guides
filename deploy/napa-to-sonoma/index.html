Aid Stations

Half Marathon aid stations at Miles 2, 4.2, 6.4, 7.7, 9.5, and 11.4. Water and electrolytes at all stations; energy chews at Mile 7.7. Rosé 5K has one water station at the midway point, plus a rosé tasting station on course (21+ with ID required). Rosé 5K course time limit: 1.5 hours. Half Marathon course time limit: 3.5 hours (approx. 16:30/mile).

Recovery Food

Bite Boxes featuring locally sourced charcuterie and snacks by LaBella Charcuterie and True Story Foods. Pre-order in advance — limited quantities available on-site. Pick up at the Bite Box tent behind Sonoma City Hall at the Expo.

Strollers

Contact the race team for stroller policies specific to this event.

Dogs

Dogs are not permitted on the course or in Sonoma Plaza. Service animals specifically trained to aid a person with a disability are permitted.

Runner Drop-Off

Drop-off location: Domaine Carneros, 1240 Duhig Road, Napa, CA. All drop-offs must be completed by 6:15 AM — vehicles will not be allowed to turn onto Duhig Rd after 6:15 AM. Exit back on Duhig Rd toward CA-Hwy 121. Do NOT stop on Hwy 121 or on Duhig Rd — CHP will be patrolling and you may be ticketed. From the drop-off, participants walk approximately 0.3 miles up the path to the start at Cuvaison Estate Wines. Note: parking is not available at Huckleberry Breakfast & Lunch or Best Western Inn at the Vines.

Spectators

Spectators

The half marathon course runs through rural vineyard roads, so spectator access along the route is limited. Best place to cheer is at the finish line at Sonoma Plaza, which is open to the public. The Wine Festival area is also public — guests without a tasting pass can enjoy the music, food, and atmosphere.

⚠

There is no parking at the start line at Cuvaison Estate Wines.

⚠

Duhig Rd will not allow vehicle access after 6:15 AM.

⚠

Do not park or stop on Hwy 121 or Duhig Rd — CHP is patrolling.

Shuttle Access

Spectators can take the pre-race shuttle to the start line (runners get priority). Return shuttles run from Downtown Sonoma to Best Western Inn at the Vines (Napa) every 30 minutes, 9:30 AM – 12:30 PM.

Post-Race

Post-Race

The finish line is at Sonoma Plaza, followed immediately by the post-race Wine Festival (9:30 AM – 12:00 PM). Half Marathon and Pairing participants receive wine festival access with their entry. The Pairing combo medal is available at the Pairing Combo Medal tent in the Race Expo after completing the half marathon — medals must be collected on-site and will not be mailed. Age group and overall awards are available for both the Rosé 5K and the Half Marathon.

Wine Festival

Wine Festival

Date

Sunday, July 26, 2026

Hours

9:30 AM – 12:00 PM

Location

Sonoma Plaza, 453 1st St E, Sonoma, CA

The post-race Wine Festival features tastings from 30+ Napa and Sonoma Valley wineries, live entertainment, exclusive photo ops, and your commemorative wine glass to take home. Access is included with all Half Marathon and Pairing entries. Rosé 5K participants may purchase a discounted festival pass at registration. Note: the Rosé 5K is on Saturday; the Wine Festival is on Sunday.

Tips & Info

  • ►

    Entry to the festival grounds is free and open to the public. Tastings are exclusive to Wine Festival Pass holders — but everyone can enjoy the music, food, and atmosphere.

  • ►

    Guest passes: all race participants can purchase up to 2 guest wine festival passes while supplies last. Add at registration or via your ACTIVE MyEvents account.

  • ►

    Wine glass pick-up and festival access begins at 9:30 AM on Sunday at Sonoma Plaza. Bring your race bib or guest pass ticket.

  • ►

    All participants 21+ must show ID to receive a wristband before tasting. Save time by getting your wristband at the ID Check station on Friday or Saturday.

  • ►

    Dogs are not permitted in Sonoma Plaza per City of Sonoma ordinance. Service animals only.
